        The Research Software Asia Australia (RSAA) flagship conferences are annual, online conferences that bring together researchers, software developers, and industry professionals to discuss the latest advancements in research software. These conferences provide a platform for knowledge sharing, networking, and collaboration among participants from diverse backgrounds.

        The conferences cover a wide range of topics related to research software, including software development best practices, reproducibility in research, data management, and emerging technologies. Attendees can expect to engage in workshops, presentations, and panel discussions led by experts in the field.

        Whether you are a researcher looking to enhance your software skills or a developer interested in contributing to research projects, the RSAA flagship conferences offer valuable opportunities for learning and professional growth.
